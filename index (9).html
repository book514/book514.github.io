How I work now

AI-assisted curriculum design

I use AI to build at a scale one designer could not reach alone. Every unit is planned, reviewed and revised against the standards and what works in real classrooms.

Engaging, not read-alouds

Lessons built around discussion, guided notes, investigations and practice, so students do the thinking.

Math in small steps

Each skill split into clear, guided steps with worked examples, so students and families can follow it.

Matched workbooks

A short teacher guide with timed lessons and answer keys, and a student workbook with no answers.

Curriculum as a website

Maps and family guides published online by grade, so everything is in one place.
