How I work now

AI-assisted curriculum design

I use AI to build at a scale one designer could not reach alone, while every unit is planned, reviewed and revised against standards and classroom reality.

Lessons

Engaging, not read-alouds

Lessons built around discussion, guided notes, investigations and practice, so students do the thinking instead of listening to the textbook read aloud.

Math

Math broken into small steps

Each skill split into clear, guided steps with worked examples and plenty of practice, so students can follow it and families can help at home.

Workbooks

Teacher guides and student workbooks

Matched sets: a short teacher guide with timed lessons and answer keys, and a student workbook with no answers.

Websites

Curriculum as a website

Curriculum maps and family guides published as websites organized by grade, so teachers and parents can find everything in one place.
